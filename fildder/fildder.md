# 配置
## PC端配置
### 点击Https
> Trust Fildder CA   
> Capture HTTPS Traffic  
> Ignore sever certificate errors

### 点击Connections
> Port  
> Enable Http/2 support  
> Allow remote devices to connect  
> Keep it on after app restart
